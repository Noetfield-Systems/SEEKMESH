SEEKMESH / NETWORK RULES

Participation terms.

Effective: November 15, 2026. SeekMesh is a developing discovery network. Its source listings are descriptive, are not endorsements, and may not reflect a provider's current status.

Community participation

Submit accurate public sources you are authorized to share. Do not submit malicious links, impersonate a contributor, post private personal information, or automate low-quality referrals. SeekMesh may reject or remove listings or Guild posts that do not meet its editorial standards. Guild members can share plain-text discovery signals and report inappropriate posts; moderators can hide content. Avoid promotional spam, unlawful content, harassment and personal contact information in Guild discussions.

Reputation and rewards

Approved resource contributions may receive off-chain, nontransferable reputation points. Points are not currency, cryptocurrency, securities, money or a promise of future tokens, airdrops, cash or profit. The team can adjust the criteria and remove fraudulent credits. A future onchain token, if any, is a separate decision requiring a published design.

Optional EVM wallet link

Linking a wallet requires an EIP-191 signed message proving control of the public address. It is not a wallet-based account login, token claim, transfer, financial transaction, custody agreement, or investment authorization. Never disclose wallet seed phrases.

Links and external content

External websites, protocols and projects are outside SeekMesh's control. Verify destination URLs and independently assess any financial products, software downloads or smart contracts.

Availability

This launch is provided on an evolving basis. Features, categories and resources may change. No promise of uninterrupted service, investment returns or official endorsement is made.
