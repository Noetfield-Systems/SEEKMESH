◇ EXPLORER'S GUIDE

Need a guide?
Start here.

Every part of the Realm has a purpose. Get a direct answer and go straight to the right place.

Where should I begin?

Create your free Explorer account, enter Season Zero, and try Daily Discovery. Everything you earn is saved.

How do I sign in or sign out?

Use the account menu at the top right. Choose Create Account, Sign In or Sign Out. A verified EVM wallet can also sign you in.

What happens when I connect a wallet?

Your wallet signs a readable message to prove ownership. Signing in requires no transaction, token purchase or gas. An optional wallet link does not transfer funds.

Are Genesis Drops a token airdrop?

No. Drops are internal activity points today. No SEEK token has been issued, and earning Drops does not guarantee a future token allocation.

What is Agent Atlas—and how can I use it?

It's a discovery graph for AI agents and tools. Search source-linked MCP, ERC-8004 and x402 entries; distinguish registry listings from actual ownership and endpoint checks.

How can I earn through referrals and missions?

Invite qualified contributors. You receive internal bonus points when your invitee submits a source that is reviewed and accepted—not just for registration.
