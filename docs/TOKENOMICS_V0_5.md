# SEEKMESH Economy — founder design, not a token launch

## Two distinct units now
- XP: non-transferable lifetime contribution and participation score. Idempotent grant entries; never redeemable for money.
- MESH Credits: non-transferable, first-party utility. Earned for activities; spent on clearly-labelled placements of one's own reviewed resources.

## Token thesis — only if the platform earns demand
Proposed ticker: SEEK (provisional, unissued).
Proposed supply ceiling: 1,000,000,000. Do not communicate this as an existing onchain asset, market value or commitment to users.

Proposed responsibilities:
- Native machine-to-machine access to discovery/provenance APIs, possibly through x402 (stablecoin checkout first).
- Provider or curator staking to deter low-quality submissions and malicious sources, only with measurable slashing rules and transparent appeals.
- Allocation of verified contribution grants from a published, capped treasury; not pay-per-click incentives.
- Community feature governance while editorial safety and source provenance remain consistent.
- Opt-in premium discovery services: project/category evidence, discoverability, uptime and provenance.

## Proposed allocation — NOT issued or ratified
- Community and proof-of-discovery contributors: 40%
- Ecosystem growth, builder grants: 20%
- Core team with meaningful long vest: 15%
- Treasury / operating reserves: 10%
- Strategic integrations: 10%
- Liquidity and deployment reserve: 5%

## Fee-aware economic design
Sponsor payment / agent API revenue -> operating costs + verification cost -> contribution budget -> sustainable rewards.
Limit actual paid rewards to realized net revenue and treasury funded pool. No fixed token-to-credit exchange and no expectation of future airdrop from XP.

## What shipped
Daily/once/quality-gated quests + XP + spendable off-chain credits + paid-intent capture (not an actual financial checkout).

## What has not shipped
ERC-20 mint or token sale; liquidity/staking; credential NFT; wallet ownership verification; confirmed Stripe payment/first customer revenue; revenue-backed cash rewards; token payout; public onchain proof.

## First real value benchmark
After 1 approved source, a builder can spend its 20 earned credits to place its approved project in the real, visible Spotlight module for 7 days, within 6 total slots. This is a user-facing benefit independent of speculative token value.
