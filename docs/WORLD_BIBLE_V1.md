# SEEKMESH — World & Narrative Bible v1.0
**Independent original fantasy universe · Creative direction, not proof of shipped game mechanics**

## Primary story
The internet has become a sky of disconnected islands. Useful tools, independent builders and emerging communities are hidden in the mist. The **Sakura Nexus** is an ancient living map; every verified discovery strengthens its branches. An explorer doesn't win by clicking or shilling—they find something real, leave a traceable beacon, and make the realm more navigable for everyone.

**Tagline:** Discover the next internet. Build your place in it.
**Secondary:** Find it. Prove it. Share it.
**Promise:** Every glowing portal leads to a real action; a beautiful world must not hide a nonfunctional interface.

## The world
1. **Sakura Nexus / Home:** luminous world tree, moonlit sky, crystal navigation, personalized account and verifiable activity counts.
2. **Cloud Atlas / Explore:** floating islands arranged by category, source cards with evidence and active links.
3. **Trail Board / Missions:** scrolls, progress, milestones, season quests, review requirements and clear reward unit.
4. **Guild Camps / Guilds:** community lodges, founders, members, discussions, genuine group signals.
5. **Crystal Forge / Build:** a builder publishes a linked project, proves ownership in future versions, and sees acceptance/revision.
6. **Treasure Vault / Rewards:** earned non-cash Credits and future escrow-funded claim receipts distinctly labelled.
7. **Moon Market / Market:** discovery placements, funded expeditions, paid API services and verified settlements where live.
8. **Explorer Lodge / Identity:** pseudonymous profile, real XP, reputation, verified wallet; no implied ownership of unminted assets.

These are SEEKMESH archetypes, **not Axies**, no Axie morphology, species genetics, battle rules, asset metadata, branded gameplay, or copied interface images. They are story/UI assets, not issued NFTs or guaranteed collectible ownership.

## Visual identity locked to the founder's approved image
**Palette:** royal midnight navy / clear cobalt sky / pale cloud blue / pearl ivory / blush sakura / lilac violet / muted moss green / restrained antique gold. Light is warm and natural, not over-saturated neon. Interactive UI is translucent glass with gold focus edges. Hero shows moving sense of depth only when performance/accessibility permits.
**Composition:** horizontal nav, central illustrated realm, left positioning + CTAs, right real account panel, row of six game-like category portals, bottom database-backed activity strip. Avoid generic dashboard cards as the opening experience.
**Motion:** gentle clouds, particle glow, drifting leaves, ambient water, hover depth. Respect reduced-motion and avoid severe layout shift.
**Accessibility:** real text and controls, keyboard operation, labels, alt text and WCAG-friendly contrast; do not bake actionable menus into a giant background image.

## Season One concept without fake prizes
**Season 0 — The First Beacons** (working dates Nov 15–Dec 15, 2026): discover, source-verify, join a guild, curate, publish an accepted contribution. Reward: ledger-backed XP and first-party Credits. If future sponsors fund prize pools, separate onchain campaign pages must disclose wallet, escrow, amount, proofs, deadline and settlement.
Later seasons can revolve around agent ecosystems, builder expeditions, guild archives and interoperable onchain badges.

## Gameplay integrity
- A quest is a unit of useful work, not a marketing CTA with a false completion receipt.
- Guilds compete on accepted quality-adjusted discoveries, not message spam.
- Wallet connections are voluntary; no wallet is required merely to explore.
- Character skins, artifacts, future NFTs and token drops are not assumed to exist until issued.
- An AI worker proposing a listing is not a human contributor and receives no human referral rewards.
