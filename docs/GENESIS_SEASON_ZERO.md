## In-app Drops
These are non-transferable internal activity points, not SEEK tokens, money, a tradable asset, or a guaranteed future token allocation.
An Explorer earns 3 Drops upon signup, 5 for the one-time proof primer, 1 for a daily check-in, 12 for an original source accepted by moderation and 8 for a qualified referral (only after a real invitee completes their first accepted source submission). The original XP and MESH Credits balances are separate. All events are persisted to SQLite and deduplicated by ledger id.

## Referral and progress
Invites use /?invite=CODE and open free registration. Wallet-native Sign-In with Ethereum or password-based login restores persistent XP, credits and Drops. The Genesis Vault contains a personal history of qualified deposits. No referral bonus is awarded for empty signups.

## Synthetic QA Arena
On first production startup the system seeds 12 scripted customer personas for six roles. They simulate signup, tutorial, daily activity, fictitious proof review, referral branching and saved progress. Each QA account has is_synthetic=1 and can never login with a real member session. There are no public fake source contributions or actual transactions.
Real user counts, real qualification totals, public XP leaderboard and real Drop supply exclude the simulation. A separate labeled Demo scoreboard is visible to visitors. Authenticated founder /operator provides all synthetic runs, QA event history, per-persona progress and a button to run another 12 synthetic journeys (300 max). These are scripted QA personas, not independent LLM browser workers or real customers.
