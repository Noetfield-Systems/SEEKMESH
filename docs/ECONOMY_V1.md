# SEEKMESH — Native Web3 Economy v1.0
**Design specification only for onchain components · Canonical status: spec/seekmesh.ssot.json**

## Three asset domains — do not merge balances
1. **XP (LIVE)**: lifetime, non-transferable, off-chain contribution score. Cannot withdraw, trade, invest or cash out.
2. **MESH Credits (LIVE)**: internal utility units. Currently earn them for verified contribution; redeem 20 for a 7-day spotlight on an approved own source, capped at six slots. No fixed conversion, token entitlement, exchange liquidity or cash-out right.
3. **SEEK (PROPOSED / NOT ISSUED)**: potential token for protocol usage and governance with measurable platform demand. Ticker, max cap, chain and allocations are provisional and not marketed as enforceable rights.

## Value that participants receive at each stage
Before money rails: credible discovery, indexed project profile, source-linked backlinks, guild connections, reputation/history, visibility redeemed via Credits.
After separately funded rails: sponsor-funded stablecoin rewards for accepted, objective quests; payout amount and escrow policy displayed before user acts.
After sustainable network: user-operated verification nodes and possible utility token, contingent on measurable demand, source quality and audits.

## Demand-side budgets and funding
A sponsor uses native wallet payment to fund an expedition with 100% reserved payout budget plus agreed protocol fee. The source of yield/rewards is **sponsor funding**, not a made-up return on deposits.
Candidate products:
- **Featured project mission**: sponsored quest where completion produces a verified source check, usability test or integration.
- **Agent-proof API**: high-fidelity source/evidence report billed by x402 over USDC per accepted job.
- **Guild expedition**: accepted guild work against sponsor-defined targets with capped spending.
- **Onchain credential**: optional mint fee covering chain gas and service overhead; not a promise of market value.
- **Marketplace services**: project source cards, documented integrations, API calls, curation services, all with transparent source and receipt.

## Economics formulas
Campaign sponsor deposit D = participant reward pool R + platform fee F + non-refunded gas/escrow reserve G. At settlement, R is only distributed by verified approved completions and is bounded by remaining escrow; unallocated R follows stated refund terms.
Operator economics contribution margin CM = collected protocol fees + paid API receipts + delivered-service receipts − verified worker inference/egress costs − payment/chain gas subsidies − refunds/chargebacks − direct verification expense.
A campaign does not prove positive profit if only gross deposits are counted: held reward principal is a liability, not revenue.
Participant stablecoin claim is **zero** until a sponsored reward budget is settled and eligibility/payout receipts exist.

## Token design — proposal, not deployed
Working supply cap **1 billion SEEK**, with allocation scenario: 40% community proof/verification, 20% ecosystem/agent grants, 15% core team vesting, 10% protocol treasury, 10% integration incentives, 5% reserve/liquidity. No presale, valuation, contract, wallet claim, market listing, yield, burn rate, or mainnet issuance has been verified.
Potential genuine utilities: paying for agent-level search/retrieval, anti-spam curator staking after objective fraud proofs, governance of public source categories/fees, capacity/agent-work deposits after measured demand. Avoid "stake for guaranteed passive income." Treasury keys, mint/upgrade rights, vesting cliff, anti-Sybil eligibility, and independent contract audit must be written before issuing tokens.
**Stablecoins first** removes the need to launch a speculative asset to prove useful Web3 commerce.

## Phase-by-phase permissions
- V0.9: real XP + Credits, reviewed submissions, in-product spotlight utility.
- Next: complete SIWE authentication and verified project ownership / review receipts.
- Native commerce MVP: Base testnet USDC (candidate), escrow + funded quests, x402 payments, receipt indexing; then live funded campaign only with explicit chain/treasury.
- SEEK optional token: after network demand and governance legitimacy, not a mandatory launch prerequisite.

## Anti-farming / reputation budget
Unique evidence hashes, cooldowns, no auto-pay for registration, referral reward only after an accepted original contribution, independent reviewer policies, duplicate detection, per-source trust ceilings, clawback/appeal for demonstrable misconduct and onchain claim idempotency.
Viral growth may not consume a financial reward budget faster than contracted funding. Airdrops, if ever created, require separate terms; XP is not a future distribution receipt.

## Wallet and chain
Chain selection **NOT RATIFIED**. Evaluate Base initially because EVM infrastructure and x402/stablecoin tool support exist, but SEEKMESH must remain discovery-agnostic across chains. Tokens/cash remain in user's self-custody until a valid authorized payment to a disclosed contract; platform never requires seed phrases.

## Explicit risk / legal accuracy
Real money deposits, token distributions and paid staking may raise jurisdiction-specific consumer, payments, tax, money-transmission and securities requirements depending on structure. None is bypassed by calling it a game. Distinguish real tasks/paid services from betting, staking investments or earnings claims. Scope compliance to the actual product and location instead of using unsupported blanket "compliance holds."
